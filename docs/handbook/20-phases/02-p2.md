---
title: P2 — Geometry certification
summary: Is this surface one sheet of papyrus, or two welded together by a segmenter that lost the thread?
---

A gate, not a measurement. It produces a verdict per surface.

## Before you start

One or more surfaces with no verdict yet. An **imported** surface qualifies too,
and usually needs this more than a grown one: growing certifies at finalisation,
importing skips that entirely, so a catalogue surface arrives unmeasured.

## The decisions

1. **Set the batch size and queue.** It runs over surfaces with no verdict. A
   bounded batch stays reviewable; an unbounded one produces a wall of verdicts
   nobody reads.
2. **Use dry run first on a new scroll.** It lists what would be certified and
   stops, so you see the population before spending the fleet on it.

## Fields

| Field | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `scroll` | restricts the batch to one scroll; empty means every scroll in the mission |
| `limit` | how many surfaces |
| `dry_run` | list and change nothing |

## What the gate actually measures

Not a scorecard, and not a set of independent tests ANDed together — an
**ordered cascade**. The first condition that fires decides the verdict, so a
surface with three problems is named for the first one in this order:

| Order | Fires on | Verdict | Reason recorded |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | interpenetration pairs, or lamina step discontinuities | `GEOMETRY_REJECTED_LAMINA_SWITCH` | `NON_PARALLEL_STAB_OR_STEP_DISCONTINUITY` |
| 2 | near-coincident overlap pairs | `GEOMETRY_REJECTED_BRIDGE` | `NEAR_COINCIDENT_DOUBLED_SURFACE` |
| 3 | a self-intersection | `GEOMETRY_REJECTED_DISTORTION` | `SELF_INTERSECTION` |
| 4 | fold-back area over the ceiling | `GEOMETRY_REJECTED_DISTORTION` | `FOLD_BACK_AREA_ABOVE_CEILING` |
| 5 | coverage failures | `GEOMETRY_REJECTED_COVERAGE` | the failing checks, listed |
| 6 | a detector did not run | `GEOMETRY_UNMEASURED` | `INCOMPLETE_<stage>` |
| — | nothing fired | `GEOMETRY_CERTIFIED` | `NO_HARD_DEFECT_AT_ARTIFACT_SAMPLING` |

Four rejection states, six reasons. The reason is the useful half: two surfaces
can both be `GEOMETRY_REJECTED_DISTORTION` and have nothing in common.

> **Note** Row 6 is the fail-closed one, and it is why unmeasured exists as a
> state. No detector fired — but one did not *run*, so the surface has not been
> certified against the defects that detector would have found. Silence is not
> a pass.

### Fold-back is a fraction, not a flag

This is worth understanding before you argue with a verdict. Fold-back is
measured as the **fraction of area** that folds, and compared against
`maximum_fold_back_fraction`, which is 0.05.

It used to be an angle test, and that rejected wrinkles: the largest area this
gate would certify on a fitted winding was half a square centimetre, against
the four a campaign asks for. No angle separates a wrinkle from an error. What
the ceiling catches is a mesh folded *everywhere*, which is the failure that
matters.

### The knobs, and which move with the scroll

| Policy | Default | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `maximum_fold_back_fraction` | 0.05 | the ceiling above |
| `minimum_valid_fraction` | 0.25 | how much of the grid must carry data |
| `minimum_largest_component_fraction` | 0.90 | one sheet, not a constellation |
| `minimum_valid_triangles` | 64 | below this there is nothing to measure |
| `band_cells` | 4 | derived from `band_um` (480) and the cell step |
| `resolution_limit_voxels` | 16.0 | derived from `resolution_limit_um` (150) |

The last two are **derived from the scroll** rather than frozen: pass the voxel
size and the inter-lamina spacing and the gate recomputes them. That matters
because a grid whose step is at or above the inter-lamina spacing cannot
certify against a lamina switch at all — it cannot see one.

> **Trap** Fold-back and the contact detectors are not relaxed per lane or per
> corpus. A calibration that let those through would stop protecting exactly
> where it is right.

## Reading the result

A surface is in one of three positions and the middle one is easy to miss:

- **Certified** — the gate looked and found no hard defect.
- **Rejected**, with a name — it looked and found one.
- **Unmeasured** — nobody looked.

> **Trap** Unmeasured is not a pass. Its QC job is created `WAITING_GEOMETRY`
> rather than `PENDING`, and the model can only claim `PENDING`, so the surface
> waits instead of quietly proceeding. A verdict promotes it the moment one
> arrives — waiting is not the same as stranded.

There are **two independent QC axes** and both must pass before anything
downstream may use a surface: geometry, and physical support — does the CT
actually contain material along it. `GEOMETRY_CERTIFIED` alone is not
admissible. A surface can be geometrically immaculate and still be a bridge
between two laminae.

## Seed agreement

A separate question from certification, and a separate state: run the same
target with two seeds and ask whether the two surfaces agree.

The number reported is a Chamfer distance, **decomposed** into a component along
the surface and one along its normal, and the headline is the **normal** one —
it answers at what depth the ink is being sampled. It is normalised by lamina
thickness, not winding pitch.

> **Note** Three numbers are reported and the total is never shown alone. A
> total of 121 µm over a 371 µm winding pitch is 0.33; a normal of 12 µm over a
> 35.5 µm sheet thickness is 0.338. They round alike and mean different things,
> so the unit says which one it is.

> **Trap** A Chamfer of zero is an error, not a pass. It means the two runs were
> the same run — usually a seed override that never took. `SEED_UNPAIRED` is a
> state, not missing data.

Seed agreement measures **reproducibility, not correctness**. Two fits can agree
perfectly on the wrong sheet.

## Traps

> **Trap** Reading certification as "this is correct". It means no hard defect
> was found at the sampling the gate uses.

> **Trap** Assuming an imported surface was checked because it is in the
> catalogue. It arrived with a bounding box and a hash and no verdict.
